---
description: Cut a Major.Minor release from the Increment
argument-hint: [version]
---
Using the **Agile-Ledger** skill, cut a release in `Releases.md`. With no argument, sweep everything currently in the 🚧 Increment into a new numbered version; `$ARGUMENTS` overrides the computed number. Increment to existing functionality ⇒ Minor; new capability/epic ⇒ Major. Move the released lines into a new dated version-history entry (newest at top) that cross-references the bundled story and epic IDs, and leave the Increment clean.
